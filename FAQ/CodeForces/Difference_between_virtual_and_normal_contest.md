The main difference between a virtual contest and a normal (live) contest (such as on [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com)) is that a normal contest happens live at a fixed global time with a brand-new problem set, while a virtual contest lets you replay a past contest on your own schedule using an old problem set. [1, 2] 
## Key Differences

* Timing & Flexibility:
* Normal Contest: Has a strict, live start and end time that all participants must follow together.
   * Virtual Contest: Can be started at any time of your choosing, giving you the same time limit (e.g., 2 hours) once you hit start. [1] 
* Problem Sets:
* Normal Contest: Uses a fresh, unseen set of problems.
   * Virtual Contest: Uses a recycled problem set from a previous normal contest. [1] 
* Official Rating Impact:
* Normal Contest: Results and scores affect your official competitive rating.
   * Virtual Contest: Does not change your official rating. It is meant for practice. [3, 4] 
* Standings & Ranking:
* Normal Contest: You compete live against active participants in real time.
   * Virtual Contest: Your score is compared against the historical results or a separate virtual scoreboard, and it does not impact the live public standings. [1, 2] 
* Pressure & Adrenaline:
* Normal Contest: High pressure, live competition, adrenaline rush, and potential hidden test cases/system tests.
   * Virtual Contest: Lower psychological stress (though it simulates the clock), and often lacks live system test surprises or real-time adrenaline. [5]
